# ADR-0001 — Independent attempt; the teammate's branch is off-limits
Status: Accepted (2026-08-30)

## Context
`origin/mle-agent` contains a teammate's complete harness with hundreds of measured experiment cells.
## Decision
Do not read, cite, or copy from it — including its "this idea is dead" conclusions. Ground everything in `docs`,
the starter kit, the dataset, and the literature in `kb/literature/`.
## Consequences
Slower start; but the two attempts stay independent evidence, so they can be compared honestly and combined at the end.
