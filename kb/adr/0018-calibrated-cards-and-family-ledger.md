# ADR-0018 — Calibrated cards, pruned variants, validated names, and a family ledger

**Status:** accepted 2026-08-31 (after live_07; Yash's assessment of the knowledge base, relayed by the research
session, listed the weaknesses; the ledger section is written by the research session).
**Extends:** ADR-0003 (cards, not RAG), ADR-0013 (evolving menu), ADR-0014 (rules in code), ADR-0016 (campaigns).

## Context — five weaknesses of the knowledge base after seven runs

1. **Promises uncalibrated.** Card `expected_delta` ranges were paper promises: the Selector's own calibration line
   read "predicted 0.006 / 0.004 / 0.003 → realised +0.0022 / +0.0005 / −0.0003", every accepted gain in five runs
   was +0.0009 … +0.0017, and ADR-0016's family ordering keys on the highest promise — so a family whose cards
   said [0.002, 0.008] outranked one measured at +0.0009.
2. **Card inflation instead of learning.** 38 of 48 cards were dead after live_06, and the Archivist had minted six
   cards for variants of two mechanisms (the same loss at 10 % / 5 % / 2.5 %, two L2 variants, one cross variant).
3. **Diagnosis on the wrong map** — fixed by the pair-type attribution (`by_pair`, research session, in the
   integration branch).
4. **Free-text hand-offs**: card names and parent references written by one role and parsed by another
   (live_05's `"node_012"`, live_06's `"champion"`, live_06's `<card> — <variant>` names).
5. **Prose facts acted on by no code**: facts §11's oracle bounds existed as text only.

## Decisions

1. **The record replaces the promise** (`distill.calibrate()`, run at the end of every distill and by `rebuild`):
   a card with any measurement gets `expected_delta = [0, max measured seed-mean gain]` (0 if never positive), the
   basis prefixed `measured (ADR-0018)`; a card without measurements whose signal family has an oracle bound
   (`kb/methods/family_bounds.json`, from facts §11 / `kb/data/screens/CEILING.md`) has its upper capped at the
   bound, the basis citing the §11 row; every card mapped to a bounded family carries one idempotent
   `- ceiling:oracle on [stack]: BOUNDED <= … (facts §11)` Measured line. Effect on the ordering key of ADR-0016
   without new code: LightGBM 0.0000, DIN 0.0000, session features 0.0009, BPR 0.0022, L2 0.0010, recency 0.0005.
2. **Variants are measurements, not cards.** The six live_06 variant cards were merged into their bases as
   `(variant: …)` Measured lines and deleted (references scrubbed); `MEASURED_RE` accepts the variant annotation; the
   Archivist's instructions say a node whose signal is already a card's is `duplicate_of` that card. Card count
   63 → 57 with no measurement lost.
3. **No free-text card names.** The Selector's parser validates `card` of improve/retest/deepen candidates against the
   card ids (a deepen may append ` — <variant>`): one format reminder, then the name is accepted and flagged
   `card_unknown`. Parent references were already resolved in code (ADR-0014). The Planner schema (ADR-0017, if
   adopted) inherits the same validation.
4. **A family ledger** (`kb/methods/families.json`, generated by `kb/methods/ledger.py` from the cards, the screens
   and facts §11 — research session): one row per signal family with its oracle bound and source, screen gains,
   measured nodes, best measured gain, status; per card the basis class (measured / oracle / paper / analogy).
   Code reads the ledger; the cards and facts stay the narrative. Follow-ups wired against it: `_family_score`
   multiplies paper/analogy promises by the realised/predicted ratio (~0.3) and uses measured gains and bounds as
   they are; the Selector's calibration sentence is generated from the ledger like `rules_text()`; the stable prefix
   carries full text only for untried / proven / open cards and one ledger row for dead ones (prefix tokens measured
   before and after).

## Consequences

- The Selector (or Planner) is now told what the record says, not what a paper promised; the ordering of campaigns
  follows measurements and oracle bounds; a variant of a dead mechanism can no longer become a fresh-looking card.
- `calibrate()` is idempotent and cheap (57 files); it runs inside `distill`, so a run cannot leave the promises stale.
- Rule 1 is deliberately blunt: a card measured once at −0.0002 keeps upper 0.0000 until a retest on another stack
  measures better — which is exactly the retest slot's job (ADR-0004), with a reason.
